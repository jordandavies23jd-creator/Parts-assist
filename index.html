Parts Assist v0.3

Workshop-language search over publicly published Genuine Volvo Truck parts data. Not chassis-specific and not connected to IMPACT/Fusion.
UK position language: n/s = left, o/s = right.
Prototype rule: terminology identifies concepts and candidates; it never invents an authoritative part number. Loaded Volvo numbers come from publicly published Volvo/dealer materials; fitment must still be verified by chassis/VIN in the authorised catalogue.
